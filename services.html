Our Advisory Services

Service 01

1. Daily Market Research

Comprehensive Pre-Market Insights

Get Started with Daily Market Research →

Trading options without an overarching market perspective is like navigating unchartered waters without a compass. Our Daily Market Research report is published before the opening bell at 8:45 AM IST every trading day. Pre-Market Briefing

Key support and resistance pivots, expected gap openings, and implied volatility (IV) shifts.

Open Interest Heatmap

Identification of major call and put writing clusters defining the probable trading range.

Daily Trading Playbook

Clear bull/bear scenarios mapped out to prevent impulsive knee-jerk decisions at the market open.

Service 02

2. Nifty & Bank Nifty Alerts

High-Probability Index Trades

Get Started with Index Alerts →

Index options require deep liquidity and rapid momentum to overcome option decay. Options Trading focuses exclusively on the most actively traded indices: Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty. Alerts are generated only when multiple technical factors align—such as VWAP reclaim, volume breakout, and open interest unwinding.

Selected Strike Precision

Strict selection of high-liquidity ATM and ITM contracts to minimize slippage and premium decay.

Instantaneous Delivery

High-speed push alerts sent directly to your screen with exact strike price, expiry, and direction.

Quality Over Quantity

No over-trading; alerts are issued strictly when favorable risk-to-reward parameters are met.

Service 03

3. Entry & Exit Guidance

Precision Execution Timing

Get Started with Entry & Exit Guidance →

In options trading, entering too early leads to theta erosion, while entering too late exposes you to sudden pullbacks. Our Entry & Exit Guidance provides unambiguous buy zones and staggered profit-booking thresholds. We clearly articulate whether to enter on a breakout or wait for a test of key intraday moving averages.

Clear Price Windows

Defined entry bands (e.g. 280 - 290) preventing members from chasing extended candles.

Partial Profit Protocols

Structured 50% profit-taking rules at Target 1 with guidance on trailing stop adjustments.

Real-Time Exit Updates

Prompt notifications if market dynamics change or momentum stalls before reaching target.

Service 04

4. Stop-Loss & Target Planning

Capital Preservation Framework

Get Started with Stop-Loss Planning →

The cornerstone of long-term trading survival is capital preservation. Options Trading enforces a non-negotiable stop-loss structure on every alert. We calculate technical invalidation levels on the underlying index chart rather than arbitrary points, ensuring your stop-loss reflects genuine market reality rather than market noise.

Hard Stop-Loss Rules

Pre-set price boundaries designed to cap potential loss before taking any trade position.

Multi-Tier Target Levels

Realistic target milestones (T1, T2, T3) grounded in average true range (ATR) calculations.

Trailing Stop Disciplines

Clear instructions to move stop-loss to cost once Target 1 is attained, eliminating downside exposure.

Sustainable Portfolio Growth

Get Started with Risk Management →

A trading strategy is only as powerful as the risk management framework supporting it. Without strict position sizing, even high accuracy cannot withstand inevitable market drawdowns. We provide tailored allocation guidelines, maximum drawdown limits per session, and mathematically proven position-sizing frameworks.

Position Sizing Formulas

Drawdown Mitigation
