Data Protection Standards

Privacy Policy

Options Trading is committed to protecting your privacy and handling personal client data in compliance with Indian information technology regulations.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit optionstrading.in, submit an inquiry, or subscribe to our advisory packages, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact Identifiers: Your full name, telephone / WhatsApp contact number, email address, and state of residence.
  • Communication Records: Messages, queries, feedback, or advisory requests submitted through our contact forms or customer desk.
  • Technical Data: IP address, device type, operating system, browser specifications, and anonymous browsing telemetry collected through cookies to optimize user experience.
  • Billing & Transaction Details: Payment confirmation identifiers and billing records. We do not store sensitive credit/debit card numbers or net-banking passwords on our servers; payments are processed securely through certified payment gateways.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate advisory and business operations:

  1. To deliver subscribed market research reports, trade alerts, and educational analysis.
  2. To communicate important regulatory notices, subscription renewal alerts, and customer service updates.
  3. To respond to your inquiries via phone, email, or WhatsApp.
  4. To comply with legal, tax, and regulatory compliance obligations in India.

3. Information Sharing and Non-Sale of Data

Options Trading does not sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal data to any third-party marketing firms or data brokers.

Information may be disclosed solely under the following limited circumstances:

  • Trusted Service Providers: Secure payment processors, cloud hosting vendors, and telecommunications providers who assist in service delivery under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Statutory and Regulatory Mandates: When required by court order, law enforcement authorities, or regulatory bodies such as SEBI under applicable Indian statutes.

4. Data Storage and Security Controls

We enforce multi-tiered technical, managerial, and physical security measures—including SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption, role-based access permissions, and firewalls—to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses minimal, non-intrusive cookies to remember user preferences, facilitate smooth navigation, and compile aggregate usage analytics. You may configure your web browser to decline cookies, though certain site features may have reduced functionality.

6. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to review, update, or request the correction of any personal data we hold about you. You may also opt out of non-essential marketing communications at any time by contacting our support desk.
