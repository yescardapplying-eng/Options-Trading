SEBI Mandated Risk Disclosure on Derivatives

As per SEBI study dated January 2023 on "Analysis of Profit and Loss of Individual Traders dealing in Equity Futures and Options (F&O) Segment":

  • 9 out of 10 individual traders in equity Futures and Options segment incurred net losses.
  • On an average, loss makers registered net trading losses close to ₹50,000 during FY 2021-22.
  • Over and above the net trading losses, loss makers expended an additional 28% of net trading losses as transaction costs.
  • Those making net trading profits incurred between 15% to 50% of such profits as transaction costs.

1. High Financial Risk of Derivatives & Options

Trading in derivatives (Options and Futures) involves substantial risk of financial loss and is not appropriate for all investors. Option contracts are wasting assets characterized by exponential time decay (Theta). If the underlying index (Nifty 50 or Bank Nifty) does not move in the anticipated direction within the expected timeframe, the option premium can rapidly depreciate to zero, resulting in a total loss of the invested capital.

2. No Guaranteed Returns or Assured Profits

Financial markets are inherently volatile and uncertain. Any examples, mathematical risk-to-reward ratios, or research case studies presented on this website are purely for analytical and illustrative purposes.

Any historical analytical accuracy, past trade reviews, or testimonial experiences referenced across our publications do not constitute a warranty or indication of future outcomes. 4. Leverage and Volatility Risks

Options provide significant financial leverage, allowing a trader to control high contract values with a small outlay of premium. While leverage can magnify percentage gains when market momentum is favorable, it equally magnifies the velocity of capital loss. Sudden adverse gaps at market opening, unanticipated monetary policy decisions, or geopolitical surprises can trigger severe stop-loss slippage.

5. Execution, Liquidity & Technological Risks

All advisory alerts generated by Options Trading are non-discretionary recommendations. Execution remains the sole responsibility of the subscriber. You may experience:

  • Slippage: Discrepancies between the recommended entry/exit price and the actual execution price filled by your broker due to rapid bid-ask fluctuations.
  • Connectivity Latency: Telecommunication outages, internet lag, or mobile network delays affecting the transmission or receipt of real-time alerts.
  • Brokerage Outages: Technical glitches, downtime, or margin square-off policies enforced by your chosen stockbroker.

6. Risk Capital Assessment

You should only trade options with capital that is specifically designated as risk capital—funds that you can afford to lose entirely without compromising your family's financial security, standard of living, emergency reserves, or long-term solvency. Never borrow funds, utilize personal loans, or trade with money earmarked for essential household obligations.
